# Supermarket Grocery Sales Dashboard | Retail Sales Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes supermarket grocery sales data from 2015 to 2018 to identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, compare product categories and regions, and discover opportunities for business improvement.

Using Excel for data analysis and dashboard development, this project transforms raw retail data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Track yearly and monthly sales and profit trends.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions and cities.
- Identify locations with high sales but comparatively low profit margins.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve retail performance.

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- **Total Sales:** 14,956,982
- **Total Profit:** 3,747,121.20
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 25.05%
- **Total Sales Records:** 9,994
- **Best Performing Year:** 2018, with sales of 4,977,512.
- **Highest-Sales Category:** Eggs, Meat & Fish, generating 2,267,401 in sales.
- **Highest-Profit Category:** Snacks, generating 568,178.85 in profit.
- **Leading Region:** West, with sales of 4,798,743.
- **Improvement Opportunity:** Oil & Masala recorded the lowest category profit margin at 24.43%.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Review pricing, product costs, and discounts for categories with lower profit margins.
- Investigate low-margin cities to identify opportunities for profitability improvement.
- Use monthly sales trends to support inventory planning and resource allocation.
- Compare product categories using both total profit and profit margin.
- Validate regional data quality before making decisions based on regional comparisons.
